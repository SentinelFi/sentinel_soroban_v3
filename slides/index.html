SENTINEL

Decentralized parametric flight-delay insurance on Stellar.
Fixed payouts, automatic eligibility, fully collateralized on-chain.

LIVE ON STELLAR TESTNET
The problem

Flight disruption is routine.
Compensation is not.

0
% OF FLIGHTS ARRIVE LATE

Roughly one in five flights is delayed — a constant, measurable, insurable risk.

Claims are manual

Traditional travel insurance means forms, receipts, adjusters and weeks of waiting for an outcome the airline's own data already proves.

Most never claim

Opaque terms and claim friction mean a large share of entitled passengers simply give up — value that never reaches the people who paid for it.

The solution

Insurance that settles itself.

Sentinel is parametric: the policy pays a fixed amount when a measurable condition is met — the flight lands late beyond a set threshold, or is cancelled. No forms, no adjusters, no discretion.

  • Fixed terms up front. Premium, payout and delay threshold are on-chain before purchase.
  • Oracle-attested outcomes. Real flight arrival data decides — not a claims department.
  • 100% collateralized. Every payout is locked in the vault at the moment of purchase. Claims can never exceed reserves.
How it works

One premium in. One of two outcomes out.

✈

Buy cover

A traveler pays a small USDC premium for a specific flight and date — e.g. 50 USDC for 500 USDC of cover.

▣

Collateral locks

The full payout is instantly reserved from the risk vault. The policy is solvent by construction.

◎

Oracle attests

Flight data executors record scheduled and actual arrival on-chain. The threshold does the judging.

◈

Auto settlement

A keeper classifies and settles every flight. No human in the payment loop.

Delayed or cancelled

The traveler claims the fixed payout — immediately, in USDC.

On time

The premium becomes yield for the underwriters who backed the risk.

Two-sided market

Travelers buy certainty.
Underwriters earn the premium.

Travelers

Transparent fixed-payout protection, purchased in one transaction and claimable in one transaction. Terms live on-chain — the product cannot quietly change after purchase.

Underwriters

Deposit USDC into an ERC-4626-style risk vault and hold yield-bearing shares. Premiums from on-time flights accrue to the share price; a FIFO withdrawal queue guarantees orderly, settlement-safe exits.

The protocol itself takes no custody shortcuts: capital sits in the vault, payouts come from locked collateral, and the books must balance on every settlement.
